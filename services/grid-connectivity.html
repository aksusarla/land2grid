Grid connectivity

A confirmed grid path, not a hopeful one.

Connectivity is where timelines actually slip: applications filed against the wrong substation, capacity assumed rather than confirmed, or approvals that stall with no one tracking them. We run this as a project in its own right.

Land management Grid connectivity Customer acquisition

Connectivity applications (CTU/STU)

We prepare and file connectivity applications with the Central or State Transmission Utility, depending on project size and location, and manage every clarification the utility raises along the way.

  • Application preparation against the correct CTU or STU process for your state
  • Technical documentation aligned to what the utility actually asks for
  • Responding to utility queries without losing your place in the queue

Substation and evacuation planning

Before we file anything, we confirm the substation actually has spare capacity and that the evacuation route is realistic, not assumed from a published capacity table that may already be out of date.

  • Substation shortlisting based on confirmed, not published, headroom
  • Evacuation route and line-length assessment
  • Flagging augmentation requirements early, before they become a cost surprise

Liaison with transmission utilities

Connectivity approvals move faster with a consistent point of contact on the utility side. We maintain that relationship across the life of the application, rather than starting from zero at every stage.

  • Single point of contact with the relevant transmission utility
  • Coordination across state and central bodies where a project spans both
  • Escalation when an application stalls without explanation

Approvals tracking through to charging

An approval isn't the finish line. We track the process through to actual charging, the point at which the connection is live, flagging any conditions attached to the approval before they affect your construction schedule.

  • Milestone tracking from application to final approval
  • Coordination of conditions precedent to charging
  • Status reporting so you always know where the application stands

Not sure what capacity is actually available?

Tell us your target substation or region and we'll tell you what's realistic before you file anything.
