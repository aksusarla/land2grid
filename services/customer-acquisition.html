Industrial customer acquisition

Power without a buyer is just a liability on the balance sheet.

Land and connectivity only pay off once there's a signed offtake behind them. We find and qualify industrial buyers, and stay in the room through PPA negotiation.

Land management Grid connectivity Customer acquisition

Open access and captive demand mapping

We map which industrial consumers in your target states are actually eligible for open access or group captive structures, and which ones have load profiles and consumption patterns that make the economics work.

  • State-wise open access and group captive eligibility screening
  • Load profile and consumption analysis against your generation profile
  • Prioritised target lists, not generic sector lists

Outreach to industrial consumers

We approach qualified consumers directly, explain the commercial case in terms their finance and procurement teams respond to, and carry the conversation through to serious interest.

  • Direct outreach to shortlisted industrial consumers
  • Commercial framing built around the buyer's own cost of power
  • Qualification before a deal reaches your team, so you're not chasing cold leads

PPA structuring and negotiation support

We help structure the power purchase agreement around tenor, tariff escalation, curtailment and banking terms that work for both sides, and support negotiation through to signature.

  • Term sheet structuring aligned to your project economics
  • Negotiation support on tariff, tenor and curtailment terms
  • Coordination with legal counsel through to execution

Power trading expertise on call

Where a deal involves banking, merchant sale or short-term trading alongside the core PPA, that expertise is available as part of the engagement, not as a separate specialist you have to find yourself.

  • Guidance on banking arrangements and settlement mechanics
  • Merchant and short-term sale options where they strengthen the deal
  • Regulatory interpretation on power trading rules specific to your state

Have capacity to place?

Tell us the volume, state and timeline, and we'll tell you what kind of offtaker and structure is realistic.
