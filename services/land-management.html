Land management

Clean land is the foundation. We don't let it become the delay.

A parcel that looks fine on a map can take months to clear once title, survey and the landowner's own expectations enter the picture. We run that process so it doesn't block everything downstream of it.

Land management Grid connectivity Customer acquisition

Site identification and screening

We shortlist parcels against the criteria that actually determine whether a site is bankable: contiguity, access, soil and slope suitability, and proximity to a substation with real spare capacity. Sites that look attractive but fail on grid proximity get filtered out before they cost you diligence time.

  • Parcel shortlisting against your capacity and technology requirements
  • Cross-checked against nearby substation headroom, not just distance on a map
  • Preliminary ownership and encumbrance checks before you commit resources

Landowner outreach and negotiation

Land in India is rarely owned by one party with one clear intention. We handle the outreach, the explanation of what the project means for the landowner, and the negotiation itself, so terms are set before legal drafting starts, not discovered during it.

  • Direct outreach to landowners and local intermediaries
  • Negotiation of lease or sale terms aligned to your project economics
  • Managing expectations on timelines, payments and land-use restrictions

Title, survey and documentation

This is where land deals usually fail after the fact. We coordinate the title search, survey and documentation work needed to get a parcel to a state your legal and lending teams will actually accept.

  • Title search and chain-of-ownership verification
  • Survey coordination and boundary confirmation
  • Document assembly for internal legal and lender due diligence

Lease or purchase coordination

Once terms are agreed, we see the transaction through, coordinating registration, payment schedules and any conditions tied to connectivity or construction milestones.

  • Coordination through registration and handover
  • Structuring payment schedules against project milestones
  • Flagging land-use or access conditions before they become construction-stage problems

Have a region in mind?

Tell us the states and capacity you're targeting, and we'll tell you what's realistic on land and grid before you commit further diligence budget.
