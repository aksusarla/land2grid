What We Do

Land, grid and a buyer — run end to end, by one team.

A project needs clean land, a confirmed connection and a signed offtaker before it's actually real, and each of those usually comes from a different vendor who only owns their own piece. We run all three ourselves, so nothing stalls waiting for someone else to pick up the phone.

Land management

Clean land is the foundation. We don't let it become the delay.

A parcel that looks fine on a map can take months to clear once title, survey and the landowner's own expectations enter the picture. We run that process so it doesn't block everything downstream of it.

Site identification and aggregation

Finding land that qualifies on paper is the easy part. The sites that actually work are rarely a single plot. They're assembled from several adjoining holdings, and getting there without one holdout undoing the whole parcel takes patience and local relationships. We run that process end to end, filtering for contiguity, access, soil and slope suitability and confirmed substation proximity before stitching fragmented ownership into one usable site.

  • Parcel shortlisting against your capacity and technology requirements
  • Aggregation of multiple adjoining holdings into a single contiguous site
  • Cross-checked against confirmed substation headroom, not just distance on a map

Negotiation and risk clearance

Most land problems surface after money has moved, not before. We handle landowner outreach and negotiation directly, and we clear title, encumbrance and ownership issues upfront so a dispute doesn't resurface mid-construction. Nothing goes to legal drafting until the risk on the parcel has actually been worked through.

  • Direct outreach and negotiation with landowners and local intermediaries
  • Title search, chain-of-ownership verification and encumbrance checks
  • Resolving disputes and competing claims before they become construction-stage problems

Purchase or long-term lease, structured around your project

Land can be acquired outright or secured on a long-term lease, and the right structure depends on your capital position and how the project is financed. We set terms accordingly and stage payments against milestones, so capital goes out as risk actually comes off the table rather than all at once at signing.

  • Outright purchase or long-term lease, structured to suit your balance sheet
  • Payment schedules staged against registration, diligence and construction milestones
  • Coordination through registration and handover

State-by-state regulatory navigation

Land law in India is set at the state level, and it varies sharply in practice, not just on paper, from Rajasthan to Madhya Pradesh to Andhra Pradesh to Karnataka to Tamil Nadu. A single national playbook doesn't hold up here. We work with people on the ground in each state, so the process reflects how land actually changes hands locally rather than how a checklist says it should.

  • State-specific land law, tenancy rules and land-use conversion requirements
  • Local legal and administrative relationships in each target state
  • Flagging state-specific risk before it shows up in diligence
Grid connectivity

A confirmed grid path, not a hopeful one.

Connectivity is where timelines actually slip: applications filed against the wrong substation, capacity assumed rather than confirmed, or approvals that stall with no one tracking them. We run this as a project in its own right.

Substation-level technical assessment

We evaluate candidate substations at a technical level, not from a published capacity table. That means checking available bay capacity, transformer headroom and actual line length to site, so a filing only goes in against a substation that can genuinely take the load.

  • Substation shortlisting based on confirmed, not published, headroom
  • Bay capacity and transformer headroom assessment
  • Evacuation route and line-length assessment

Load flow study and connectivity applications

Before any application is filed, we work through the load flow study the discom will eventually run, so there are no surprises when connectivity is granted or denied. Applications go in against the correct CTU or STU process for your state, with the technical documentation the utility actually asks for.

  • Pre-filing load flow assessment aligned to how the discom will evaluate the application
  • Application preparation against the correct CTU or STU process for your state
  • Responding to utility queries without losing your place in the queue

Charges and liaison with transmission utilities

Connectivity comes with a running list of charges: deposit work, augmentation costs and wheeling charges among them. Approvals also move faster with a consistent point of contact on the utility side. We manage both sides of this, tracking what's owed and when, and maintaining the utility relationship across the life of the application rather than starting from zero at every stage.

  • Tracking and managing connectivity, augmentation and wheeling charges
  • Single point of contact with the relevant transmission utility
  • Escalation when an application stalls without explanation

Right of way, ours to solve

Right of way for the transmission line connecting a project to its substation is often the single biggest delay on a project timeline, and it's usually left for the developer to work out alone. We take responsibility for it instead: securing the right of way and resolving the disputes that come with it, so it never sits on your critical path.

  • Securing right of way for the connecting transmission line
  • Resolving disputes with landowners along the route
  • Tracking right of way status alongside the connectivity application itself

Approvals tracking through to charging

An approval isn't the finish line. We track the process through to actual charging, the point at which the connection is live, flagging any conditions attached to the approval before they affect your construction schedule.

  • Milestone tracking from application to final approval
  • Coordination of conditions precedent to charging
  • Status reporting so you always know where the application stands
Industrial customer acquisition

Power without a buyer is just a liability on the balance sheet.

Land and connectivity only pay off once there's a signed offtake behind them. We find and qualify industrial buyers, and stay in the room through PPA negotiation.

Open access and captive demand mapping

We map which industrial consumers in your target states are actually eligible for open access or group captive structures, and which ones have load profiles and consumption patterns that make the economics work.

  • State-wise open access and group captive eligibility screening
  • Load profile and consumption analysis against your generation profile
  • Prioritised target lists, not generic sector lists

Outreach to industrial consumers

We approach qualified consumers directly, explain the commercial case in terms their finance and procurement teams respond to, and carry the conversation through to serious interest.

  • Direct outreach to shortlisted industrial consumers
  • Commercial framing built around the buyer's own cost of power
  • Qualification before a deal reaches your team, so you're not chasing cold leads

PPA structuring and negotiation support

We help structure the power purchase agreement around tenor, tariff escalation, curtailment and banking terms that work for both sides, and support negotiation through to signature.

  • Term sheet structuring aligned to your project economics
  • Negotiation support on tariff, tenor and curtailment terms
  • Coordination with legal counsel through to execution

Power trading expertise on call

Where a deal involves banking, merchant sale or short-term trading alongside the core PPA, that expertise is available as part of the engagement, not as a separate specialist you have to find yourself.

  • Guidance on banking arrangements and settlement mechanics
  • Merchant and short-term sale options where they strengthen the deal
  • Regulatory interpretation on power trading rules specific to your state

Land, connectivity, a buyer, or all three — tell us the state, capacity and stage you're at, and we'll tell you what's realistic before you commit further diligence budget.
