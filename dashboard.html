Project Dashboard

A searchable view of our pipeline, coming soon.

We're building a dashboard where IPPs, EPCs and landowners can browse land parcels, connectivity status and offtake-ready sites directly, instead of going back and forth over email. It isn't live yet — this page is holding its place.

What it will show

A filterable list of sites and deals we're tracking — by state, capacity, connectivity status and stage — so you can see what's actually available instead of asking us to compile it each time.

  • Land parcels by state, size and aggregation status
  • Grid-connectivity stage for each site (application, approval, charging)
  • Offtake-ready capacity looking for a buyer or a developer

In the meantime

Tell us what you're looking for — a parcel in a specific state, a connectivity slot, a PPA counterparty — and we'll work it the same way we do today: directly, over a call or email, against our live deal list.

We'll let people on our contact list know as soon as this is live.
