# Service page banners

Each of the three service pages (`services/land-management.html`,
`services/grid-connectivity.html`, `services/customer-acquisition.html`) has
one static photo banner under its page header — a single real photo rather
than the homepage's rotating video, to keep subpages lighter.

1. Land management — "Scenic aerial view of vibrant green fields with a
   winding stream cutting through" — Pexels
   Page: https://www.pexels.com/photo/732547
   File: https://images.pexels.com/photos/732547/pexels-photo-732547.jpeg

2. Grid connectivity — "Detailed view of high voltage electrical substation
   with transformers and power lines" — Pexels
   Page: https://www.pexels.com/photo/236089
   File: https://images.pexels.com/photos/236089/pexels-photo-236089.jpeg

   Romania, reflecting in water on a sunny day" — Pexels
   Page: https://www.pexels.com/photo/2183113
   File: https://images.pexels.com/photos/2183113/pexels-photo-2183113.jpeg
