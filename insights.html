News & Insights

We don't have a blog yet. Here's where to find us.

land2grid doesn't run a content calendar. When we do publish something — a note on a regulatory change, a teardown of a tender, a lesson from closing a deal — this is the page it lands on first.

What this page will carry

Short, specific write-ups tied to work we're actually doing — not generic renewable-energy content. Think: how a particular state's open access rules changed, what a connectivity application actually took to clear, or what a captive structuring negotiation turned on.

  • Regulatory and tariff updates across the states we work in
  • Notes from live land, connectivity and offtake engagements
  • Occasional teaching material, in the same spirit as our leadership's existing market-education work

In the meantime

Our leadership already shares market knowledge outside this site — through a power-trading and regulation channel and a set of online courses, as described on the About page. We haven't linked those here yet; ask us directly and we'll point you to the right one.

Have a specific question about Indian solar, wind or grid-connectivity regulation? That's usually faster to answer directly than to wait for a post.

Have a question we should be writing about?

Tell us what you're trying to figure out on a live project, and we'll either answer directly or it becomes the first thing we publish here.
