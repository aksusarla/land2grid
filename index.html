Why land, grid and offtake have to move together.

Land, grid connectivity and industrial offtake are usually handed to three different parties, and the handoffs between them are where projects lose months: land gets secured before anyone confirms the substation has headroom, or a PPA gets negotiated before connectivity is filed. Coordinating the three from day one removes that fragmentation, cuts execution friction, and reduces the commercial uncertainty that stalls a project between milestones.

How an engagement runs.

The same five steps, whether you're an IPP, an EPC, or a C&I buyer securing clean power directly.

  1. BriefYou share capacity, technology, target states and timeline.
    • Capacity and technology: solar, wind or hybrid
    • The states you're targeting
    • Any land or connectivity work already in motion, so we don't duplicate it
  2. ShortlistWe screen land parcels against grid proximity and available capacity.
    • Substation headroom checked against confirmed capacity, not just published numbers
    • Parcels filtered on contiguity, access, and soil and slope suitability before any site visit
  3. SecureWe close land and file connectivity in parallel.
    • Landowner negotiation and documentation run alongside the CTU/STU filing, not after it
    • Title, survey and connectivity milestones tracked against one shared timeline
  4. MatchWe connect project supply with industrial demand and support PPA discussions.
    • Counterparties shortlisted against your profile, not a generic list
    • Generation profile for a developer, demand profile for a buyer
    • Term sheet structuring and negotiation support through to signing
  5. Hand overYou receive the finished package: land, connectivity and a signed power agreement.
    • One package: secured land, a confirmed connectivity approval, and a signed PPA
    • Status reporting throughout, so nothing arrives as a surprise at handover
