Independent power producers who want a pipeline of ready land, a confirmed grid path and signed offtake, without running three parallel workstreams.

Contractors who need land and connectivity resolved before construction starts, and customers to bring to the table for build-own-operate or sale models.

How an engagement runs.

  1. BriefYou share capacity, technology, target states and timeline.
  2. ShortlistWe screen land parcels against grid proximity and available capacity.
  3. SecureWe close land and file connectivity in parallel.
  4. MatchWe bring industrial customers and support PPA discussions.
  5. Hand overYou receive a project with land, connectivity and offtake in place.
