Privacy

Privacy Policy.

Last updated 15 November 2026. land2grid is an early-stage business, and this page is a plain-language explanation of what this website does with your information — not a substitute for legal advice specific to your situation.

What this site collects

This website collects information in two ways:

  • Forms you submit. The contact page and the footer enquiry form ask for your name, email address and a message about your project. We use this only to respond to you.
  • Cookies and local storage. See the Cookie Policy for the full detail — in short, we store a small amount of data in your browser to remember your cookie choice and whether you've dismissed the chat prompt. We do not currently run analytics or advertising cookies on this site.

How form submissions are handled

Form submissions on this site are processed by Netlify Forms, a third-party service that receives the data you submit and forwards it to us by email. We don't store submissions in a database of our own. We use the details you send only to reply to your enquiry and don't sell or share them with other third parties for marketing.

Hosting and basic logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub's infrastructure may log standard technical information (such as IP address and browser type) for security and performance purposes. We don't have access to, or use, this data ourselves.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and site data for land2grid at any time through your browser settings, and you can change your cookie consent choice from the Cookie Policy page. If you've sent us a message and would like it deleted from our records, email us and we'll action that request.

Changes to this policy

As land2grid grows and adds tools like analytics or a proper CRM, this policy will be updated to reflect that, and the "last updated" date at the top will change.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy or how your information is handled, write to hello@land2grid.com.
