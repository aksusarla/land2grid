Careers

No open roles right now — but tell us where you'd fit.

land2grid is a small, early-stage team. We're not running a formal hiring process today, so there's no job board here to browse — just an honest read on who we'd want to hear from when that changes.

What we look for when we do hire

  • Direct experience in Indian renewable energy, power trading or grid connectivity — not adjacent sectors
  • Comfortable owning a workstream with real ambiguity, not just executing a defined brief
  • Able to work directly with landowners, transmission utilities or industrial consumers, not just analyze from a desk

How to reach out

If that sounds like you, a short note on what you've actually worked on — a connectivity application you filed, a PPA you negotiated, land you closed — gets read faster than a generic resume. Send it to the email below; we'll keep it on file and reach out if something opens up that fits.
