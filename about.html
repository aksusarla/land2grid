About

Why land2grid exists.

Most renewable projects in India stall for the same three reasons: land that isn't clean, a grid connection that isn't confirmed, or a buyer that isn't locked in. land2grid exists because those three problems are usually handed to three different people, and the handoffs are where projects lose time.

Power sector practitioners, not generalist consultants.

The land2grid team brings 18+ years of experience in the Indian power sector, spanning renewable energy development, power trading and business development. That experience sits across the three functions a project actually needs: sourcing land that will survive diligence, reading transmission capacity correctly before filing, and knowing which industrial consumers are genuinely in the market for power.

Our leadership has helped execute more than 2 GW of solar and wind projects across India, working directly with IPPs and EPCs rather than observing the sector from the outside. That track record shapes how we screen a parcel, how we frame a connectivity application, and how we structure a PPA so it survives negotiation.

We also teach this market.

Beyond project work, the team shares market knowledge through the Electricity Market in India channel and a set of online courses on power trading and regulation. That teaching discipline keeps the regulatory and tariff knowledge current across states, which is the same knowledge we apply to every parcel, connectivity filing and offtake deal we work on.

It also means we're used to explaining complex regulatory positions in plain language, to developers, financiers and customers alike. If your board or lenders need the land, grid or offtake position explained clearly, that's a normal part of how we work.

How we think about the work.

One accountable owner

You deal with one person who owns land, connectivity and offtake together, not three vendors who each own a third of the problem and none of the outcome.

Sequenced, not siloed

Land, grid filings and customer outreach run in parallel on purpose, because each one changes what's realistic for the other two.

Built on real deal flow

Our process comes from having actually closed land, filed connectivity and negotiated PPAs, not from a generic project-management template.
