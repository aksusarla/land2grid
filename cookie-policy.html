Cookies

Cookie Policy.

Last updated 15 November 2026. This page explains the cookies and browser storage this site uses; there are only a few, and none of them are for advertising.

What we use, and why

  • Cookie consent choice: saved in your browser's local storage once you accept or reject, so we don't ask again on every visit.
  • Chat prompt dismissal: saved for your current browser session, so the "have a project in mind?" prompt doesn't reappear on every page once you've closed it.

Both of these are strictly necessary for the site to remember your preferences; they don't track you across other websites, and we don't use them to build an advertising profile.

What we don't use

This site doesn't currently run analytics, advertising or third-party tracking cookies. If that changes as land2grid grows, this page will be updated to say so before any such cookie is set.

Change your choice

You can reopen the cookie banner at any time and pick Accept or Reject again.

Browser controls

You can also clear cookies and local storage for this site at any time through your browser's own settings, which will reset the choice above and sign you out of any saved preference.

Questions

For anything else about how this site uses cookies, see the Privacy Policy or write to hello@land2grid.com.
